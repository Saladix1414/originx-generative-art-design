# Phase 10 — Canonical Promotion Boundary

Phase 10 defines the boundary for promoting a candidate artifact into canonical status.

Promotion is a decision record only. It does not move image files, execute models, upload assets, or publish final artwork.

A candidate can be promoted only when:

- candidate and canonical identifiers are present
- provenance manifest digest is valid
- required quality gates passed
- failed required quality gates are empty

Blocked promotions preserve the failed required evidence so later phases can repair the candidate without losing traceability.
