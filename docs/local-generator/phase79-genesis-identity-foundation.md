# Phase 79 - Legendary Genesis identity foundation

Introduces deterministic production identities for Genesis tokens #0001
through #0010.

Historical RARE/Primitive Origin first-ten fixtures remain unchanged and are
not production NFT records.

The public collection era is `PRIMORDIAL ORIGIN`. The existing canonical
Structure Engine internally maps `LEGENDARY` to its versioned
`Ascended Origin` technical profile. Both values are recorded explicitly;
neither is silently substituted.

No artwork is generated in this phase. SD 1.5 remains a candidate pending the
Genesis benchmark and manual Quality Gate.
